# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 15, 2026

Pops ("we", "our", or "the app") is an iPhone app that teaches German vocabulary through short animations, word puzzles and chats. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

## No Sign-Up Needed

You can use Pops without creating an account. When you first open the app, it is given a random ID. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details.

## Information We Collect

- **Learning progress and settings:** what you have learned, your answers, your streak and daily goal, and the choices you make in the app, such as your native language and level.
- **Usage and diagnostics:** how the app is used, including recordings of in-app activity, crash and error reports, and basic device information such as the device model, iOS version and app version.

This information is linked only to your random ID. Some settings and cached content are stored only on your device.

### Advertising and Tracking
Pops does not show ads. It does not use Apple's Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## How We Use Your Information

- To save your progress and pick the next words for you
- To show your streak, daily goal and translations in your native language
- To find and fix problems and to improve the app
- To check that your app version is still supported

We do not sell your information and do not share it with anyone for advertising.

## Service Providers

We use trusted third-party providers to host the app's data and to process usage and diagnostic information. They process data only on our behalf, to run the app, and some of them may process it in the United States.

## Data Storage and Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections and protected so that your progress can be accessed only from your app.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is kept until you delete it. You can delete it at any time in the app: open your profile and tap **Konto löschen** (Delete account). This permanently deletes your data and your progress from our servers. If you delete the app without doing this first, you can ask us to delete your data by emailing us.

## Your Rights

You can:
- Delete your data directly in the app
- Ask us for a copy of your data, or for its deletion, by email
- Ask us to stop collecting usage data for your ID

## Children's Privacy

Pops is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete their data.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are shown here, with a new "Last updated" date.
