# Pops — FAQ

_Last Updated: 2026-11-15_

---

## Getting Started

### What is Pops?
Pops teaches German words through short animations. You spell each new word on a letter wheel, answer quick picture and article quizzes, and use the words in short chats. You can learn in one endless feed, topic by topic, at your own pace.

### Do I need an account?
No. Pops works right away without signing up. Your progress is saved under a random ID.

### Which devices are supported?
Pops is available for iPhone.

### What level is Pops for?
Pops starts from zero (A1) and goes on to intermediate topics. When you first open the app, pick "Anfänger" (beginner) or "Mittelstufe" (intermediate) and it starts at the right place.

---

## Using the App

### Why is the app in German?
The app is in German so that you learn the language while you use it. Word meanings, topic names and chats are translated into your native language, which you can change at any time in your profile.

### How do I change my daily goal or native language?
Tap the profile icon on the home screen. There you can change your daily goal (Tagesziel), your native language (Muttersprache) and the sound effects.

### Can I pick a topic myself?
Yes. On the home screen, tap "Alle Themen" to see every topic. Tap one to start it.

### Does the app work offline?
Pops needs an internet connection to load new words. If you lose your connection, the sets you finish are saved on your iPhone and sent as soon as you are back online.

### How do I delete my data?
Open your profile and tap "Konto löschen". This permanently deletes your progress and your data. It cannot be undone.

## Troubleshooting

### I found a mistake in a word or translation. How do I report it?
Use the contact form on this page and choose "Mistake in a word or translation", or email us at **cleevao@gmail.com**. Tell us the word and your native language.

### A word's picture, video or sound does not load. What should I do?
Check your internet connection and close and reopen the app. If it keeps happening, contact us.

### How do I contact support?
Email us at **cleevao@gmail.com**. We aim to respond within 24 hours.
